# RECON

RECON is a student-led national workshop on system security, focused on moving from vulnerabilities to trustworthy systems through hands-on technical events, operational discipline, and real-world security thinking.

The program combines flagship cybersecurity competitions with structured operations playbooks, sponsor and community participation, and a campus-wide execution model designed to scale safely.
