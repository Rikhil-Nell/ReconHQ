## Messaging

### One-liner

> A national-level, DEFCON-style cybersecurity fest — 3 days of CTFs, KOTH, 10+ villages, and hands-on security at VIT-AP.

### Key Points

- Security-first, technically credible, university-hosted.
- Overnight CTF and KOTH competitions with real prizes.
- 10+ side events: hardware hacking, OSINT, forensics, AI red-teaming, gaming, and more.
- 600 participants, 50 workforce, sponsors from across India.
