## Recovery Playbook (Time Slip)

- **Slip ≤ 7 min:** compress transition buffers
- **Slip 8–20 min:** cut non-essential stage filler
- **Slip > 20 min:** invoke session swap with backup block

## Stage Roles Per Session

|Role|Responsibility|
|---|---|
|Emcee|Stage flow, transitions, crowd engagement|
|Timekeeper|Countdown cards: T-10, T-5, T-2, CUT|
|AV Operator|Audio, visuals, screen cues|
|Speaker Handler|Speaker prep, mic check, green room|
|Floor Manager|Seating, crowd, entry/exit management|

>[!danger] **No session runs unless all five roles are present.**
